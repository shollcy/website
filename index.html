About

I am an economist and Senior Research Fellow at the National Institute for Legislative and Democratic Studies (NILDS), Nigeria's National Assembly institute, where I provide research and policy support to the Nigerian parliament. I am also a researcher with the Firoz Lalji Institute for Africa at the London School of Economics, part of the LSE–Africa Climate Foundation team working on trade and climate, and I teach on the NILDS–University of Benin postgraduate programmes.

My work sits at the intersection of energy, climate, trade and development policy, with a focus on Africa. I use computable general equilibrium (CGE) models, macro-econometrics and policy evaluation to ask how public policies and economic shocks, from fuel-subsidy reform and carbon border adjustments to the African Continental Free Trade Area, affect growth, welfare, food security and distribution.

I hold a doctorate in Applied (Energy) Economics from Xiamen University, China, where I was affiliated with the China Center for Energy Economics Research. I also hold an M.Sc. in Economics from the University of Lagos, a B.Sc. in Economics from the University of Ilorin, and a graduate certificate in Applied Development Economics from Université Laval, Canada.

Research

Energy & climate policy

Renewable energy transitions, just energy transition pathways, energy subsidies and the economics of decarbonisation in Africa.

Trade policy & AfCFTA

The African Continental Free Trade Area, carbon border adjustment (CBAM) and the trade–climate nexus for African economies.

Public financial management

Fiscal policy, budgeting and appropriation, fiscal consolidation and the role of the legislature in economic governance.

Sustainable development

Food security, climate disasters, youth employment and the demographic dividend in Nigeria and sub-Saharan Africa.

Distributional impacts

Economy-wide and household-level effects of public policy and economic shocks, from minimum wages to global price shocks.

Methods CGE modelling GTAP Macro-econometrics Impact evaluation GAMS Stata EViews
